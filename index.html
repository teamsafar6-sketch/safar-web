Visa & Travel Requirements

Essential entry rules, baggage allowances, and transit checklists for Afghan & international passport holders.

UAE (Dubai / Sharjah)

30-day & 60-day tourist visas available through approved UAE sponsors. Passport must be valid for at least 6 months with confirmed return ticket.

Turkey (Istanbul)

Sticker visa required prior to travel for Afghan ordinary passports; e-Visa eligible if holding valid US, UK, Schengen, or Ireland visa/residence.

Saudi Arabia (Umrah & Transit)

Umrah visas issued via Nusuk-authorized operators. Free 96-hour Stopover Visa available when flying Saudia or Flynas.

Domestic Flights (Kam Air & Ariana)

Carry original Electronic Tazkira or valid Passport. Arrive at Kabul, Herat, Mazar, or Kandahar airport at least 2 hours before departure.
